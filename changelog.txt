Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-08
  Features:
    - Auto belt planner: click two points to route a belt between them.
    - Ghost placement for robots, or free placement (can be disabled per map).
    - Belts layout (undergrounds for obstacles) and Undergrounds layout (chains of maximum-length pairs).
    - Routes prefer few turns, run beside existing belts and keep clear of machines.
    - Long routes are calculated in the background with a bounded work budget per tick.
